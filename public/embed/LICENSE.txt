Search model: a reduced copy of minishlab/potion-retrieval-32M
(https://huggingface.co/minishlab/potion-retrieval-32M, snapshot 6fc8051f), licensed MIT by
its authors (Minish Lab), per the model card's "license: mit".

Changes: PCA from 512 to 256 dimensions and int8 quantisation per token row, by
tools/build_embed_model.py in this repository. The vocabulary is the model's own.
